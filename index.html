Most common roof problems in Deer Park

What goes wrong on Deer Park roofs

Ceiling stains & attic leaks

The stain shows where the water ended up, not where it got in. Water can travel along rafters before it drips — so we trace the leak to its source.

Hail & wind damage

Houston gets over 100 thunderstorm days a year. Hail bruises shingles and wind creases tabs — damage that is easy to miss from the ground.

Cracked pipe boots

Rubber pipe boots crack after 8–12 years in Houston's UV. On roofs over 10 years old, a cracked boot is the number-one leak source — and a small fix.

Age & Houston weather

Intense UV, humidity and heavy rain wear Houston roofs faster than in drier climates. Most last 15–20 years — and the last few years are when the leaks start.

Insurance — plain English

Will insurance cover this?

Sudden damage — hail, wind, a fallen tree — is usually covered by Texas homeowners insurance. Wear, age and slow leaks usually are not. And a denied claim still sits on your record, so a small repair is often not worth filing for.
