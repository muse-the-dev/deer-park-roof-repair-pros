Signs you need a roof repair

  • A brown stain on the ceiling, or a drip after rain
  • Shingles in the yard after wind, or bare patches on the roof
  • Granules from the shingles collecting in the gutters
  • Daylight showing through the roof boards in the attic
  • Flashing pulling away from a wall, chimney, or vent
  • A musty smell in the attic, or water around a vent pipe

If you see one of these, a free inspection will tell you whether it is a small fix or something bigger. Small leaks do not stay small.
