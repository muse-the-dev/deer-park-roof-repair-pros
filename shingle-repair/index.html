Insurance — plain English

Will insurance cover this?

Sudden damage — hail, wind, a fallen tree — is usually covered by Texas homeowners insurance. Wear, age and slow leaks usually are not. And a denied claim still sits on your record, so a small repair is often not worth filing for. If your damage came from a storm, see our storm & hail damage page for the full plain-English guide, including deductibles.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

How much to replace a few shingles?

A small shingle patch in Houston runs about $150–$600 (RISE; Tell Project). Some Houston roofers list a single shingle replacement from $225–$250. Your price depends on how many shingles, how high the roof is, and whether the colour can still be matched.

Can you match my shingles?

We match the colour and style as close as stock allows. Shingle colours are discontinued regularly, and sun-faded shingles will not match a new shingle exactly — we will tell you that before we start, not after.

How many shingles need replacing before it is a full roof?

There is no single number. A few missing shingles is a repair. Shingles missing across whole slopes, widespread curling, or granule loss everywhere usually means the roof is at the end of its life — and we will tell you that honestly.

Will a shingle repair stop the leak?

If the leak is caused by the damaged shingles, yes. But many leaks blamed on shingles are actually flashing or a cracked pipe boot. We check the whole area, not just the shingles you can see from the ground.

What causes shingles to curl or crack in Deer Park?

Houston heat and UV, age, and poor ventilation in the attic. Houston roofs typically last 15–20 years — shorter than in drier, cooler climates.

Do wind-damaged shingles count for insurance?

Wind damage is usually covered as sudden damage — but a creased shingle needs documenting, and your deductible decides whether a claim pays anything. If the damage came from a storm, see our storm & hail damage page.
