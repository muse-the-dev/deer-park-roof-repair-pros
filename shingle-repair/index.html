Shingle repair in Deer Park

Most homes in Deer Park have asphalt shingles. Texas sun, wind, and hail wear them down — edges curl, granules wash off, and shingles lift or blow away. One missing shingle is a small fix. A whole slope of curled shingles is a bigger problem, and we will tell you which one you have.

Signs you may need this repair

  • Shingles in the yard after wind
  • Bare patches where shingles are missing
  • Curling or buckling shingle edges
  • Granules collecting in the gutters
  • Cracked shingles around vents or valleys

Repair cost depends on the size of the damage, the roofing material, and how easy the roof is to access. The inspection and estimate are free — you will know the price before any work starts.
