Insurance — plain English

Will insurance cover this?

Sudden damage — hail, wind, a fallen tree — is usually covered by Texas homeowners insurance. Wear, age and slow leaks usually are not. And a denied claim still sits on your record, so a small repair is often not worth filing for. Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

How much does roof leak repair cost in Houston?

Most Houston-area leak repairs run $150–$1,500, with a typical job $650–$900 (RISE, Mar 2026; Angi Houston average $1,002, Jul 2026). A single small issue is often $350–$900. Your price needs an on-roof inspection — no roofer can price a repair from a photo of a ceiling stain.

How does a roofer find the source of a leak?

By tracing the water's path: attic inspection, roof inspection, and a water test where it is safe to do one. The stain shows where the water ended up — water can travel 10–15 feet along rafters before it drips.

How do I know if my roof leak is actually fixed?

Ask for photo proof: photos of the problem, the repair and the result. We show you all three, so you can see what was done and where.

Is a small leak urgent?

Yes — a small leak that runs for months can rot the deck underneath, and that damage is usually not covered by insurance. Mould can start within a day of water getting in. Get it looked at, even if the drip is small.

Can I repair a roof leak myself?

A small, easy-to-reach shingle can sometimes be a DIY job, but most leaks are misdiagnosed — the stain is not the entry point. Climbing a wet roof is dangerous. A free inspection costs you nothing and tells you what the leak actually is.

How long does roof leak repair take?

Most single-issue repairs are done in one visit once the leak is found. Finding the leak is the slow part — that is why a proper inspection matters more than a fast patch.
