Cracked pipe boots

Insurance — plain English

Will insurance cover this?

Sudden damage — hail, wind, a fallen tree — is usually covered by Texas homeowners insurance. Wear, age and slow leaks usually are not. And a denied claim still sits on your record, so a small repair is often not worth filing for. Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What is a pipe boot?

A pipe boot is the rubber or metal collar that seals around a vent pipe where it comes through your roof. It keeps water out of the hole the pipe passes through. When the rubber cracks, water runs straight down the pipe into your attic.

How long does a pipe boot last in Texas sun?

Rubber pipe boots typically crack after 8–12 years in Houston's UV and heat. On roofs over 10 years old, a cracked boot is the number-one leak source.

My leak is around a vent — is that flashing?

Very likely, yes. Leaks around vents, chimneys and skylights are usually flashing or a pipe boot, not the shingles. That is good news — it is usually a small, cheap fix, not a new roof.

How much does pipe boot repair cost?

In Houston, pipe boot replacement runs about $150–$500 (RISE; Tell Project). Some Houston roofers list it at $225. Your price needs an inspection — if the deck underneath is rotted, the repair is bigger.

What is flashing, exactly?

Flashing is the thin metal that seals the joints of a roof — where it meets a wall, a chimney, a vent, or another slope (a valley). When flashing fails, water gets in at the joint, even if the shingles look fine.

Can a cracked pipe boot cause a big leak?

Yes. A cracked boot can let in a surprising amount of water in a Houston downpour — and because it is hidden on the roof, the leak can run for months before the ceiling stain shows up.
