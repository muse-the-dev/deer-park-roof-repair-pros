Flashing and pipe boots — the hidden leak source

Flashing is the metal that seals the joints where the roof meets a wall, a chimney, a skylight, or a valley. A pipe boot is the rubber collar around a vent pipe sticking through the roof. In the Texas sun, that rubber splits and cracks — and it is the single most common leak we find in Houston-area homes.

The leak shows up as a stain on the ceiling, often right under a bathroom vent. The shingles above it can look fine. That is why we check the boots and flashing first when a leak is near a vent.

Signs you may need this repair

  • A leak around a bathroom or kitchen vent pipe
  • A stain on the ceiling under a vent or chimney
  • Flashing pulling away from a wall or chimney
  • A cracked or split rubber boot around a pipe
  • A leak that keeps coming back after shingles were patched
