Storm and hail damage in Deer Park

Deer Park sits in the Houston area, where a 10-minute hailstorm or a hard wind gust can do real damage. Hail bruising is easy to miss from the ground — the shingles look fine, but the granules are knocked off and the roof starts leaking months later.

After a storm, we inspect the whole roof, photograph the damage, and note the date of the storm. That documentation is what an insurance adjuster will ask about. We cannot promise how a claim will turn out — insurance covers sudden events like hail and wind, not wear — but good photos and a clear date help.

Signs you may need this repair

  • Shingles lifted, creased, or missing after wind
  • Granule loss or bruising after hail
  • A limb fell on the roof, or debris hit it
  • A leak that started right after a storm
  • Dented flashing, gutters, or vents after hail

Repair cost depends on the size of the damage, the roofing material, and how easy the roof is to access. The inspection and estimate are free — you will know the price before any work starts.
